    T-00 · Briefing

    Every AI output is a draft.

    Verification is the testing that decides whether an AI output is ready to use. That goes well beyond finding errors: it asks whether the output holds up for your purpose, your audience and your stakes.

    AI can helpBuild

    Write a lesson or an email, code a website, build an interactive, design a rubric.

    →
    AI can helpTest

    Question the output and investigate its claims.

    →
    Only youSign off

    Use, modify, retest or do not use.

    Looks can be deceiving.

    Two AI outputs can look similar at a glance. The difference only shows up once you test them: checking claims, measuring against criteria, asking what's missing.

    Four ways AI output fails

    Failure 1

    Volume

    More output than anyone can realistically check. Length and polish hide gaps.

    TEST QUESTIONWhat actually matters for how I'll use it?

    Failure 2

    Hallucinations & mistakes

    Confident claims, dates, quotes or sources that are wrong or invented.

    TEST QUESTIONCould I confirm this somewhere independent?

    Failure 3

    Bias

    One-sided framing, missing perspectives, assumptions presented as fact.

    TEST QUESTIONWhose view is centered, and whose is missing?

    Failure 4

    Sycophancy

    The AI agrees with you or tells you what you hoped to hear.

    TEST QUESTIONWould it say the same if I'd asked the opposite?

    Test settings

    How hard should you test it?

    Think of something you'd actually make with AI. Set the three dials and the panel recommends a test level.

    The Human Premium
    AI can help find what deserves testing. Evidence and human judgment decide what's ready to use.
